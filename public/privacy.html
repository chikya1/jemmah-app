Jemmah Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

Overview

Jemmah is a private, offline-first personal assistant app. Your privacy is our core principle — not an afterthought.

Data Collection

Jemmah collects no data. The app does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information with any third party, including the developer.

Data Storage

All data you enter into Jemmah — notes, tasks, reminders, files, and links — is stored exclusively on your device using your browser's local IndexedDB storage. This data never leaves your device.

Internet Access

Jemmah requires internet access only to load the application itself. No user data is ever transmitted over the internet. The app functions fully offline once loaded.

Notifications

Jemmah may request permission to send you local notifications for reminders you set. These notifications are generated on your device and do not involve any external servers.

Third Party Services

Jemmah does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services.

Children

Jemmah is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will update the date above. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
