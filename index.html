All PIN values and lock preferences are stored locally on your device only. No server, no cloud, no recovery mechanism exists.

Part 2 — Privacy Policy

1. Zero Data Collection

Jemmah collects absolutely no personal data. We have no backend servers, no cloud databases, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising SDKs. You are never required to create an account or provide any personal information.

Because no personal data is ever collected or received by us, SKYLabs India Private Limited is not a "Data Fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

2. Local Data Storage

All data you enter — notes, tasks, calendar events, passwords, account entries, chat history, media files — is stored exclusively in a private SQLite database and AsyncStorage on your device. This data is inaccessible to other apps and cannot be read externally unless your device has been rooted or compromised.

If you activate the App Lock feature, your chosen 4-digit PIN and biometric preference are processed entirely on-device and stored locally in isolated system registers. No network entity can access or query this security setup.

3. AI Processing

All AI inference happens entirely on your device's CPU/GPU. Your messages, prompts, and chat history are never sent to any external server — not to us, not to Google, Meta, Microsoft, or any other party.

4. Internet Usage

Jemmah uses the internet only for downloading AI model files from HuggingFace (user-initiated) and opening links you have saved in your default browser (user-initiated). No background data transmission occurs.

5. Permissions

  • RECORD_AUDIO — Declared in manifest but NOT currently active. Microphone input is disabled in this version. No audio is recorded or processed.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Local reminders and calendar alerts only. No push servers used.
  • READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — For importing AI model files from your device storage (legacy Android compatibility).
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES — For importing images into the private media vault.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT — Local device fingerprint unlock only. No security tokens are transmitted anywhere.
  • INTERNET — For model downloads and opening saved links in browser only.
  • CAMERA — Reserved for future use. Not currently active.

6. Your Right to Deletion

You have complete control over your data. To delete all Jemmah data: use "Delete All Chats" in Settings, go to Android Settings → Apps → Jemmah → Storage → Clear Data, or uninstall the app. All actions permanently and irreversibly delete all your data.

Jemmah is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Reserved for future use. Not currently active in this version.
